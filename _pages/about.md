Hi, I'm **Ping Ting** — a data analyst based in New York City.

I focus on turning messy real-world data into clear answers for business questions. My work sits at the intersection of **marketing analytics**, **business intelligence**, and **exploratory data analysis** — I'm most interested in projects where the analysis ends with a decision, not just a chart.

Right now I'm building a portfolio of projects that combine **public NYC datasets** with the kind of business framing I'd bring to an in-house analytics role. The first project, [NYC Events Market Analysis](/projects/), looks at three years of city event permits to understand seasonality, geographic concentration, and category shifts across the five boroughs.

My toolkit is **Python (pandas, scikit-learn, statsmodels)**, **SQL**, and **Jupyter** for analysis; **matplotlib/seaborn/plotly** for visualization; and **Streamlit** for shareable interactive write-ups. I'm currently expanding into BigQuery and dbt.

Before pivoting fully into analytics, I worked on business development at [Zetta Robotics](https://zetta-robotics.com), focusing on humanoid robot rentals for events in NYC — which is what got me curious about the city's events market in the first place.

If you're hiring or just want to talk about NYC, data, or robots, the fastest way to reach me is the email above.
