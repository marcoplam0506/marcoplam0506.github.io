9×a level is worth that much more than the skill

Learning SQL does not raise your price. It decides which half of the market you can be hired into.

If you are hiring

You are not paying for the skill, you are paying for the function. A data-fluent brand marketer costs what a brand marketer costs, and there are very few of them.

If you are moving

The data-heavy functions are the ones you can enter on skill alone. They are also the ones priced lowest. Enter there, then move, and do not wait for the skill to pay.

What this cannot tell you

  1. Pay is the midpoint of a posted range. Where a real offer lands inside that range is invisible here.
  2. Comms & PR holds 16 roles and Marketing Ops 14. The two ends of the cancellation sit on the two smallest cells.
  3. Functions were assigned by title rules I wrote, not by employers. Three misclassifications were found and fixed by audit; nobody else has checked them.
  4. This says data-heavy roles are priced higher inside a function. It does not say that learning the skill raises an individual’s pay.
  5. New York only. Pay is here to read because the city forces disclosure, and that is also why the finding stops at the city line.
