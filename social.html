At this size the honest deliverable is not a growth forecast. It is a list of what the next ninety days have to measure.

What the data decided

The measurement window, the topic concentration, the audience split, and the single follower drop. Four findings, each with a denominator big enough to stand on.

What I decided

Posting cadence, the content ratio, the order to fix things in. These came from experience, not from this dataset, and the report said which was which.
